Privacy policy

Lunera Smart Technologies Ltd. · Effective 2026-10-31

This policy explains what personal information we collect through lunerasmart.com, why we collect it, and how we protect it. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

  • Information you send us. When you use the contact form, you give us your name, email address and, if you choose, your company, the service you are interested in and a message. The form is delivered to info@lunerasmart.com through FormSubmit, a form-to-email service, which passes the message to us and does not use it for any other purpose.
  • Usage information. If analytics is enabled, we use Google Analytics 4 with IP anonymization to see how visitors use the site: pages viewed, approximate region, device type and referring site. This does not identify you by name.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records standard technical information such as IP address, browser and time of request to keep the site running and secure.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if you ask, to tell you when we launch.
  • To improve the website.
  • To protect the site against abuse.

We do not sell personal information.

Cookies

The site itself sets no cookies. If Google Analytics is enabled, it sets analytics cookies, which you can block in your browser or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. Fonts are hosted on this site.

Sharing

We share information only with providers that help us run the site and reply to you (website hosting, form delivery, email hosting and analytics), and only as needed. Some of these providers may store data outside Canada. We may disclose information where the law requires it.

Retention

Messages are kept as long as needed to respond and, if you become a client, for the length of our relationship and any period the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to info@lunerasmart.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

The site and the contact form are served over HTTPS. Access to enquiries is limited to the partners who answer them.

Children

This site is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new effective date.
