Questions

Before you call.

When does Lunera launch?

Our new services, website and partner program go live on December 1, 2026. Site assessments are already open for projects that cannot wait.

What does Lunera install?

AI video security, access control and identity, intrusion and duress systems, AI weapons screening at entrances, secure networks, private AI servers that keep data on the premises, and smart building systems such as lighting, climate controls, sensors, boardrooms and digital signage.

Who do you work with?

Corporate offices and industrial sites, restaurants and hospitality groups, healthcare facilities, government and municipal buildings, banks and credit unions, and property and construction companies across Hamilton, Halton and the western GTA.

How does an engagement work?

It starts with a paid site assessment: a walkthrough of your space, network and risks, followed by a written design and a fixed quote. The fee is credited against the project. Installation is done by certified technicians, and every system is covered by a monthly managed plan for monitoring, maintenance and support.

What is your hardware policy?

We do not install products from vendors restricted by the Government of Canada, including Hikvision and Dahua. Every proposal states this policy, and every camera install includes signage, a retention setting and a privacy policy template.

Do you serve homes?

Yes, for executive and custom homes, usually through the builders and firms we already work with. Our focus is commercial and institutional work.
